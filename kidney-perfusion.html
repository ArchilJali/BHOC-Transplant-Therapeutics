Kidney transplantation, perfusion and oxygen research

Kidney preservation research spans isolated organs, experimental transplantation and recipient outcomes. These settings should remain separate when an oxygen carrier or a change in perfusate is evaluated.

Name the organ model and the next validation step

An ex vivo kidney experiment can assess oxygen consumption, vascular response and measured function during perfusion. A transplant model adds a different question: how the organ performs after it returns to a recipient.

The BHOC programme starts with characterization and controlled organ-specific testing. Findings from a historical carrier or a red-cell experiment can inform that work, while BHOC-specific performance needs its own evidence.

A rat kidney-preservation study at 10°C

The historical catalogue includes kidney preservation at 10°C with Hemopure and sodium thiosulfate in a rat syngeneic orthotopic transplantation model. The named interventions and the animal model are essential parts of the record.

This study is not a human BHOC trial. The existing evidence brief links the original publication and separates the experimental finding from the BHOC development question.

Functional oxygen handling in isolated human kidneys

The RBC-storage analysis describes isolated human kidneys perfused ex vivo with differently stored donor red-cell preparations. It examines organ oxygen handling rather than a BHOC intervention.

Its respiratory-rate and oxygen-diffusion-capacity findings are separate measures. They should not be collapsed into one general improvement percentage or described as patient-transfusion or post-transplant outcomes. The source is useful for designing functional readouts.

Use historical carrier publications at their verified scope

The historical catalogue also lists a study of synthetic hemoglobin-based carriers as alternatives to packed red cells in normothermic kidney perfusion. Its single-source review status remains visible in the library.

A publication title can identify a relevant research direction. Detailed claims about benefit, safety or equivalence require the verified full study and its actual model. The shared catalogue remains the source of record.

Define the perfusate and the readouts together

A BHOC kidney study would characterize the formulation and oxygen-affinity profile under the proposed temperature, flow and gas conditions. Carrier concentration, oxygen content, extraction, methemoglobin, vascular resistance and organ-specific function should be interpreted together.

A useful collaboration identifies which question the model can answer and which subsequent validation step remains. Ex vivo function, animal autotransplantation and clinical transplantation are not interchangeable endpoints.
