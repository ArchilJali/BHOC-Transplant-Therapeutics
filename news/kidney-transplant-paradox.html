The Kidney Transplant Paradox

BHOC Transplant · 13 November 2026

Jeff A. Parke and Archil Jaliashvili’s first joint preprint, The Kidney Transplant Paradox: When Dialysis Economics, Organ Scarcity, and Medical Innovation Collide, appeared on Preprints.org on 6 November 2026.

What affects access to kidney transplantation?

The essay examines how dialysis economics, referral and waitlisting, and donor kidney shortages shape access to kidney transplantation. It discusses why some donated kidneys go unused and the role of machine perfusion in organ preservation.

The authors draw on patient experience, published research and registry reports. This version has yet to undergo peer review.
