The Oxygen Advantage in Organ Preservation

BHOC Transplant · 15 November 2026

The Cold Ischemia Foundation’s infographic The Oxygen Advantage traces a path from veterinary oxygen-carrier work to research on oxygen delivery during donor organ preservation. The accompanying LinkedIn post explains why this work matters to people waiting for a kidney transplant and to their care partners.

The Foundation explains the research in plain language and brings patients’ questions to the BHOC Transplant team. Those questions span the transplant journey, from how a donor kidney is preserved before surgery to how its function is measured afterward.

What is oxygenated machine perfusion?

Oxygenated machine perfusion uses a pump to circulate oxygen-rich fluid, called a perfusate, through a donor organ outside the body. BHOC Transplant is studying whether the Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier (BHOC) could support oxygen delivery from that fluid to organ tissue.

The infographic presents the Foundation’s perspective on potential benefits. Effects on graft function and graft survival after transplantation need to be measured in clinical studies of the specific carrier and perfusion protocol.
