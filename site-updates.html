13 October 2026 · Shared scientific evidence

Five oxygenation and microcirculation research publications added

The shared BHOC Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation library added five Dyson/Singer publications on tissue oxygenation, shock and perfusion, relevant as background to transplant oxygen-continuity research. These publications do not establish BHOC transplant efficacy. The Oxygen Diffusion & Microcirculation catalogue now lists 215 records; the five BHOC Evidence Library collections contain 923 placements in total.
